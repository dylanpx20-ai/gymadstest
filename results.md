# Results & Key Takeaways

## NYC Gym Marketing Budget — GPT-6 Assisted Monte Carlo Simulation

This project used **OpenAI's GPT-6 and Python to develop and execute a Monte Carlo simulation of 600,000 potential monthly marketing outcomes in just 11.49 seconds**. Across four marketing budget strategies and three CPC environments (Conservative, Moderate, and High), the **Balanced Omnichannel strategy consistently outperformed the alternatives**, generating the highest median number of new paying gym members and the lowest median Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). Under Moderate advertising costs, the Balanced strategy generated a median of **49 new members at a $204.08 CAC**, compared with 37 members at a $270.27 CAC for the Paid-Only strategy. This represents approximately **32% more members and a 24.5% reduction in median acquisition cost**, using the same $10,000 monthly budget.

The simulation also demonstrated how changing advertising costs can dramatically affect business performance. Under Conservative CPC conditions, the Balanced strategy generated a median of **62 new members at $161.29 CAC**, compared with 39 members at $256.41 CAC under High CPC conditions. Despite rising costs, the Balanced strategy maintained a **65.98% probability of acquiring at least 35 new members** in the most expensive environment. By incorporating randomized CPCs, conversion rates, customer behavior, and local NYC market saturation, the model provides a more comprehensive view of potential outcomes than a traditional single-number forecast. These results are based on synthetic assumptions rather than actual advertising campaign data.

## Why This Matters

The most compelling takeaway is the **speed and accessibility of AI-assisted statistical modeling**. With GPT-6 assisting in the development of the Python code, probabilistic modeling, scenario design, and visualizations, the project evaluated **600,000 simulated outcomes in approximately 11 seconds**. NumPy performed the underlying statistical computations, while GPT-6 helped translate a real-world marketing question into a reproducible analytical framework. Instead of committing thousands of dollars based solely on intuition, businesses can use this approach to compare strategies, quantify downside risk, and test changing market conditions before investing real money. **The goal isn't to predict the future perfectly — it's to use AI and Monte Carlo simulation together to make faster, more informed business decisions under uncertainty.**
